1. The Problem: The Cascade Dilemma

Modern Security Operations Centers (SOCs) run chained AI models that make automated incident decisions. When an upstream sensor or telemetry field is corrupted, spoofed, or misclassified, false decisions cascade across the organization.
❌ Traditional Full Rollback Bluntly resets the entire state. Recomputes valid decisions, causing high latency, disruption, and system-wide collateral damage.
⚠️ Manual Ad-Hoc Fixes Engineers override individual fields blindly. Creates unverified side effects and destroys forensic auditability.
✓

2. Our Solution: Selective Rewind

DECISION-REWIND solves this by introducing causal reachability and counterfactual replay. Instead of rolling back the world, it calculates precisely which decisions were invalidated and rewinds only those decisions.
🎯 100% Selective Precision Only downstream decisions that mathematically depend on the corrected feature and diverge in output are rewound.
🛡️ Zero Collateral Damage Healthy, unaffected decisions remain permanently locked and valid. State changes are verified deterministically.
⚙
